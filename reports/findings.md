## Business Interpretation

- Logistic Regression identified 78.34% of actual churned customers in the test set, but it also produced 288 false-positive alerts.
- Random Forest produced fewer false positives and achieved the highest accuracy, but it missed more churned customers and had lower recall.
- For retention operations, recall is important because failing to identify a churned customer means losing an opportunity for intervention.
- These results should be treated as test-set estimates; the model should be validated on a later time period before production deployment.
